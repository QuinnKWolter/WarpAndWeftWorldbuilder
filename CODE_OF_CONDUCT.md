# Community conduct

Treat contributors and writers with respect. Welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and creative practices. Discuss ideas with care, accept constructive feedback, and protect other people's private writing.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or suspend access when necessary to protect the community.

When the repository is published, use its private maintainer contact for conduct concerns. Do not include another person's private information in a public issue. Until then, report concerns directly to the repository owner through a private channel you already have.
